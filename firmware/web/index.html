Advanced

Both simulator modes drive no outputs except the blue LED, for testing on a DevKit. With real inputs, the contact inputs come from the real pins (LOW = closed, pull-ups on) while the gate stays simulated; tie an unused NC input (photo-eye GPIO 34, edge GPIO 35) to GND. Never choose a simulator mode on a board installed at a gate. Changing it restarts the board.
